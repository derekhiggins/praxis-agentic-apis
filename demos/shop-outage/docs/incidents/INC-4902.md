# Incident report INC-4902: slow storefront pages from product image CDN

Severity: SEV2
Status: resolved
Duration: 2 hours 5 minutes
Services: storefront

## Summary

Product pages took 6-10 seconds to render. The image CDN provider had a regional
degradation and storefront fetched product thumbnails server-side without a
timeout. Checkout was not affected.

## Evidence

- storefront access logs: `GET /products/...` with `rt=` above 6 seconds; status 200.
- No 5xx responses; checkout requests normal.
- Provider status page reported elevated latency in the region.

## Resolution

Added a 500 ms timeout and placeholder images for thumbnail fetches. Thumbnails
now load client-side.
